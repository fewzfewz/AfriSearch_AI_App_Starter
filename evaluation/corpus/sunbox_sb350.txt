SunBox SB-350 Solar Home Kit: Product Sheet

This product sheet is an illustrative sample written for testing AfriSearch. SunBox is a fictional brand.

Model: SunBox SB-350.

What is in the box. One 80 watt solar panel, a lithium battery unit with a capacity of 192 watt-hours, 4 LED lights with 5 metres of cable each, and a phone charging port.

Running time. On a full charge the kit powers all its lights for about 9 hours.

Warranty. The SunBox SB-350 has a 2-year warranty on the battery and the panel. Keep the receipt; the warranty is not valid without it.

Price. The recommended retail price of the SunBox SB-350 is 31,000 shillings.
