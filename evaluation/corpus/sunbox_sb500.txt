SunBox SB-500 Solar Home Kit: Product Sheet

This product sheet is an illustrative sample written for testing AfriSearch. SunBox is a fictional brand.

Model: SunBox SB-500.

What is in the box. One 120 watt solar panel, a lithium battery unit with a capacity of 307 watt-hours, 6 LED lights with 5 metres of cable each, and a phone charging port.

Running time. On a full charge the kit powers all its lights for about 12 hours.

Warranty. The SunBox SB-500 has a 3-year warranty on the battery and the panel. Keep the receipt; the warranty is not valid without it.

Price. The recommended retail price of the SunBox SB-500 is 47,500 shillings.
